🔥 The least charitable assessment of you

You have saved about 140 recipes and training plans over four years, and your own captions show the same three trailheads and the same twelve-week block on repeat every one of those years. That folder is not a plan, it is a shrine. Meanwhile the actual pattern here is beautifully consistent: you do things for people without being asked, and you have never once, in four years of posts, asked anyone for anything back. That is not generosity running unchecked, it is a ledger you have decided not to show anyone — which is why 'quietly tallying' shows up in your own relationship read a page ago, and why nobody around you has learned to reciprocate, because you have never given them the information required to try.

And the tell is the thing that is not here at all: forty-one posts closing out a plan, zero admitting the plan was heavy. An organiser who never once says the load is a lot is not calm, they are unwilling to find out what happens if they put it down for a week and see who notices.

💬 What an honest friend would tell you

Cook one of the hundred and forty recipes instead of saving a hundred and forty-first — the gap between the folder and the plate is the actual finding, not the folder. Unfollow the five hundred and ninety-three accounts you have not engaged with in a year; a subscription you are not reading is not community, it is guilt with a notification badge. And say the specific thing out loud to one specific person: not 'I'm fine', the actual sentence — 'I need you to plan the next one' or 'I noticed you didn't ask how my week was' — because a need that is never stated is a need nobody can meet, and you already know that, you just have not tried saying it where it could be heard.
