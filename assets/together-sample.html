What the research says
  • About two-thirds of couples' recurring disagreements are never solved, only handled well (Gottman). How a disagreement starts and whether repair attempts land matter more than how often couples disagree; one partner pushing to talk while the other withdraws is a common, breakable cycle (Christensen & Heavey).
  • Most recurring disagreements come from lasting differences in personality and needs: the goal is a way of living with them, not a winner (Gottman).
  • Behind a disagreement that keeps coming back there is often a hope or need that hasn't been said; naming it is what loosens it (Gottman, dreams within conflict).
